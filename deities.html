Our Deities

Hanuman Ji

Embodiment of devotion, strength, courage and selfless service.

Shiv Ji

Deity of transformation, meditation and inner peace.

Radha Krishna

Divine symbols of eternal love, compassion and devotion.

Satyanarayan Ji

The Deity of Truth, invoked through holy vows and prayers to bring harmony and fulfill auspicious desires.

Durga Maa

Divine mother and protector who destroys evil and grants strength.

Bal Hanuman

The youthful form of Hanuman Ji, radiating innocence, boundless energy and divine power.

Ganesha Ji

Remover of obstacles and deity of new beginnings, wisdom and prosperity.

Kal Bhairav

Fierce guardian form of Shiv Ji, protector against evil and deity of time.

Laddu Gopal

The beloved infant form of Krishna, embodying pure joy, innocence and divine grace.

Lakshmi Maa

Avatar of wealth, fortune and abundance, blessing devotees with prosperity and grace.

Navagrah

The nine celestial bodies whose cosmic energies guide destiny, karma and life's journey.

Ram Darbar

The divine court of Shri Ram — an icon of righteousness, dharma and ideal kingship.

Ram Ji

The seventh avatar of deity Vishnu, embodiment of virtue, truth and devotion to dharma.

Sankat Nashan Ganesh

The crisis-destroying form of Ganesha Ji, invoked to remove hardship and bring relief.

Saraswati Maa

Avatar of knowledge, music, arts and learning, guiding seekers toward wisdom and creativity.

Shivling

The sacred aniconic form of Shiv Ji, representing the infinite, formless source of creation.

Surya Narayan

The sun avatar, source of light, life and cosmic energy, worshipped for health and vitality.

Lakshmi Narayan

The divine union of deity Vishnu and Lakshmi Maa, embodying eternal love, abundance and cosmic harmony.
